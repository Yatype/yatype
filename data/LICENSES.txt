1. Russian word forms
---------------------

Derived from the Russian Hunspell dictionary (ru_RU.dic, ru_RU.aff) by
Alexander I. Lebedev.

MODIFIED VERSION: the dictionary was expanded into a plain list of word forms,
lowercased, with "ё" replaced by "е", proper names and abbreviations removed,
merged with the project's own word list and stored in a compressed format.
